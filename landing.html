Threat Horizon • About DRP

What is Digital Risk Protection (DRP)?

Defending what firewalls and endpoints cannot see: threats born outside your network on the public web.

Beyond The Security Perimeter

Traditional cybersecurity guards endpoints, email inboxes, and internal cloud infrastructure. However, over 80% of modern phishing campaigns, lookalike websites, and brand fraud originate on public web platforms completely outside your perimeter.

Digital Risk Protection (DRP) continuously scans the public, deep, and dark web to detect typosquatted domains, social media impersonations, leaked employee credentials, and hostile brand exploits the moment they are staged—long before malicious payloads reach your internal network.

Identifies attacker preparations weeks prior to active phishing execution.
Zero endpoint agent installations or complex firewall integrations required.
Automated legal-grade takedown requests sent directly to registrars and hosts.
